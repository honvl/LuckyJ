Privacy policy

Last updated 15 November 2026 · 日本語

This page explains what the LuckyJ Playbook site and the LuckyJ AI Mahjong Tutor collect, why, who else handles it, and how to have it deleted. Both are run by Honver, an individual. Write to luckyj@yui-life.com with any question or request. This is an independent project, not affiliated with Tencent or the makers of LuckyJ.

1. Reading the site

  • The playbook, the guide and the replays need no account. They set no cookies and run no analytics or advertising trackers.
  • Pages load their fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser sends your IP address to Google when it fetches them.
  • The site is hosted by Vercel, with a copy on GitHub Pages. Both keep standard request logs (IP address, browser, pages requested) to run and protect the service.

2. Your account

  • You sign in with Google or Discord. They send us your name, email address and profile picture. We accept only an email address the provider has verified.
  • We store your email address to find your tutor. Your name and picture stay in your sign-in cookie and are not stored by us.
  • The chat pages set only the cookies sign-in needs: your session, a security token and the page to return to. There are no tracking cookies.

3. The tutor

When you subscribe, you tell us where you play (Mahjong Soul or Tenhou) and your in-game name. Then:

  • We fetch your ranked game records under that name. A game record contains every player's plays and the other three players' in-game names, as the game recorded them.
  • The tutor is Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic. It reads your game records, your messages and the notes it keeps about your play, and writes analyses and chapters. Anthropic processes and stores this data for us, as our service provider, while your tutor thread exists.
  • Our database (Neon, in the United States) holds your account record: your sign-in email, your tutor thread, your subscription status, where you play, your in-game name and which chapters you made private.
  • Everything you type in the chat goes to the tutor. Do not send anything you would not want processed, such as passwords: the tutor never needs one and never asks.

4. Published chapters

  • Chapters the tutor writes about your play are published on this site under your in-game name, unless you choose otherwise at sign-up. You can make any chapter private from your chat page at any time.
  • Published chapters and replays show other players by seat and rank, not by name.

5. Payments

  • Payments are handled by Stripe through Link, which is the seller of record. Stripe collects your payment details, billing address and tax information and sends your receipts. Stripe’s privacy policy applies to that data. We never see your card number.
  • Stripe tells us your email address, a customer and subscription number, the subscription’s status and its renewal date.

7. How long we keep it

  • We keep your account record, tutor thread, notes and chapters while your account is open. If you cancel, they are kept so you can come back, until you ask us to delete them.
  • When you ask, we delete your tutor thread and notes at Anthropic, your account record, and the game records we fetched, and take down your chapters. Stripe keeps payment records for as long as the law requires.
  • Hosting logs are kept by the hosting providers under their own policies.

8. Your rights

You can ask to see the personal data we hold about you, to correct it, to receive a copy, to delete it, or to stop a use of it. Write to luckyj@yui-life.com from the email address you sign in with; we reply within 30 days. You can also complain to your data protection authority, for example in Japan the Personal Information Protection Commission.

Where the EU or UK GDPR applies, we use your data to provide the tutor you subscribed to (contract), to keep the service secure (legitimate interest) and to publish your chapters (your choice, which you can change). Other players’ in-game names in your game records are used only to analyse your games and are never published; a player who wants their name removed from our copies can write to us.

9. Children

The tutor is not meant for anyone under 16. If you think a child has signed up, write to us and we will delete the account.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and the date at the top. If a change affects how your data is used, we will tell subscribers before it takes effect.
