## Method

- The wall comes from the raw Mahjong Soul record (`tmp/tensoul/fetch_wall.js`). On all ten hands of this game the
  deal is wall tiles 0-51, the draws start at tile 52, the dora indicator is tile 131 and the ura indicator 130.
- The hand is replayed to your eighth discard and the local Mortal policy plays all four seats from there, your seat
  too, by its first choice. Each sample plays the cut you made (9m) and the other (2s).
- Sample 0 finishes the hand on the real wall. The other 999 reshuffle the tiles nobody had seen (the rest of the live
  wall, the ura and the closed dead wall) and keep every hand as it really was.
- The rest of the game runs on fresh walls, the same walls for both cuts in a sample, with Mahjong Soul's ranked rules:
  South 4 ends the game when someone has 30,000 and the dealer did not repeat (or repeated by winning as top), West
  hands are sudden death, below zero ends it, and leftover sticks go to first.
- Rank points are the Saint 3 Jade values read off your own results: (score - 30,000) / 1000 plus 130, 65, 0 or -250,
  rounded up.
- Engine checks: 52 Mortal self-play games ran with no errors, 10.4 hands a game, 15% draws, points always summing
  to 100,000, and someone below zero in 31% of games (29% of your 164 real games).

## Reading

The samples keep the opponents' real hands, so 9m always deals in: the comparison measures this deal, not the
decision on what you could see. The decision itself is the card's point (2s sat outside shimocha's early 3s, 9m was
covered by nothing). Both lines have Mortal playing your seat after the hand, so the 133 is the value of the one tile
with the rest of the game played the same way.
